About us

Our 16th-century pub retains its original beams, timbers and brickwork, with a bright and airy atmosphere and modern country décor.

Join us for good, honest pub food, or simply enjoy a drink and a chat with friends. We offer a fantastic selection of wine, drinks, tea, coffee and some of the finest local ales — everyone is welcome here.

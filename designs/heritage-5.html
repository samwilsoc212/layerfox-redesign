About us

Historic charm, a bright and airy welcome

Our 16th-century pub retains the charming features of its historic past, including original beams, timbers and brickwork, while also boasting a bright and airy atmosphere with modern country décor.

Join us for good, honest pub food, or simply enjoy a drink and a chat with friends. We offer a fantastic selection of wine, drinks, tea, coffee and some of the finest local ales.

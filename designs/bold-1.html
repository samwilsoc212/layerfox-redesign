About us

Old beams. Bright rooms. Everyone welcome.

Our 16th-century pub retains the charming features of its historic past — original beams, timbers and brickwork — with a bright and airy atmosphere and modern country décor.

Join us for good, honest pub food, or simply enjoy a drink and a chat with friends. A fantastic selection of wine, drinks, tea, coffee and the finest local ales.
